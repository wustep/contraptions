# Stock-timing shows

Stephen's timing rule governs Première Take B and Clair Take A. Each placement comes directly from the stock piece's `place()` function. Playback uses elapsed seconds since entry. There are no time knots, authored rests, added fuse time, altered segment durations, or speed matching at handoffs. Take A remains unchanged and stays the default.

## Plan before generation

Each work has four maps, Regular → Forest → Aqua → Arcade. Musical phrases live inside those maps. A phrase boundary is a camera/composition cue, never a reason to stop the ball or load a map. The arranger chooses stock variants and orders their durations around recording cues. Exact cue errors are reported after generation rather than concealed by retiming.

Première uses the existing Prati No. 1 recording, offset 2.38 seconds, including its final resonance. Take B covers every entry in the four stock catalogs, including the ticket finale and the short accent pieces missing from Take A. Prefer an unused piece over a repeat. The entire catalog lasts only about 2:36 at its longest stock settings; covering the full 4:50 requires additional travel. Repeated carries, climbs and descents connect the remaining music and fold the path into a readable map. Avoid repeating short accents to fill time.
