# Carmen Prelude recording

`carmen-prelude-musopen.ogg` is the 2:05 Musopen recording of Georges Bizet's
Prelude to Act I of *Carmen*. Wikimedia Commons lists the recording under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.

Source and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carmen_-_Prelude_to_Act_1.ogg

The Sol one-shot Show uses the unmodified Ogg file. The source page reports a
duration of 125.052 seconds and a file size of 1,820,266 bytes.
Its SHA-1 matches the source page: `0c6eae8d8c5f323f21ff19c18f0eacbe0a4d67c0`.
