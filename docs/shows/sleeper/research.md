# Sleeper · after Dune — research

What the show is built from: Denis Villeneuve's *Dune* (Legendary Pictures / Warner Bros., 2021; released as *Dune: Part
One*; written by Jon Spaihts, Denis Villeneuve and Eric Roth after Frank Herbert's novel; cinematography Greig Fraser;
production design Patrice Vermette; score Hans Zimmer). Compiled 21 October 2026. Claims resting on one secondhand
source are marked *(uncertain)*. Sources are listed at the end.

The title is the novel's line, *the sleeper must awaken*, which the film's first trailer made its own. Paul spends the
film between sleep and waking. On Caladan he dreams of a desert and a girl in it. In the spice he sees what is coming.
In the desert he meets her.

Part Two already has a show, **Maker** (`harkonnen-arena`). Sleeper takes nothing from it: no code, no pieces, no
shots. Maker's harvester sinks into a funnel with a ring of teeth. Here the worm rises beside the harvester like a
tower and comes down on it. Maker turns its colour down to infrared and black and white. Here the colour moves from
Caladan's grey-green to Arrakis's ochre, and Paul's visions carry the ochre in early.

## The story as the film tells it

In order, from the film's plot and the sources below:

1. **Caladan.** House Atreides holds the sea planet. Paul dreams of Arrakis and of a Fremen girl. The Emperor's herald
   gives Arrakis to Duke Leto. Paul trains with Gurney, Duncan, Thufir and Yueh.
2. **The Gom Jabbar.** Reverend Mother Mohiam tests Paul: his hand in a box of pain, the poisoned needle at his
   neck. Jessica waits outside the door reciting the Litany against Fear. He passes. His visions grow stronger after.
3. **The bull.** Paul's grandfather, the Old Duke Paulus, was killed in a bullfight. The film shows his portrait
   several times, and the head of the bull that killed him. The head hangs on the wall on Caladan, is packed when the
   house leaves, and is hung on the wall at Arrakeen. Leto and Paul talk by the sea about whether Paul wants to be
   Duke. *(The setting of that talk is uncertain: a cliff and graves over the sea in some recollections.)*
4. **The leaving and the arrival.** The Atreides leave Caladan, are carried by a Guild heighliner, and come down at
   Arrakeen. The city sits in a bowl of rock against the worms, and its buildings are angled with thickened walls.
5. **The ornithopters, the harvester and the first worm.** Leto, Paul and Kynes fly out to a spice harvester. Worm sign
   comes, and the carryall does not. The thopters land, and the crew is taken off. Paul, in the spice, sees visions and
   stands still. Leto drags him back, and the worm takes the harvester.
6. **The night.** Yueh brings down Arrakeen's shields. The Harkonnens and the Emperor's Sardaukar come in the dark.
   Yueh drugs Leto and leaves a poison-gas tooth in his mouth. Leto bites it before the Baron, and the gas kills him and
   Piter. The Baron, who floats on suspensors, escapes it.
7. **The desert.** Paul and Jessica are flown out to be left in the desert. Jessica uses the Voice, and the guards die.
   That night in a stilltent, with spice in the air, Paul sees a holy war fought in his name and the girl again.
8. **The storm.** Duncan and Kynes find them. Duncan dies holding off the Sardaukar so they can get away. Paul flies an
   ornithopter into a sandstorm to shake off their pursuers ("Fear is the mind-killer"), and comes out the other side.
9. **The Fremen.** Stilgar's troop meets them in the rocks. Chani is among them, the girl from the dreams. Jamis
   challenges Paul. Paul kills him in the duel, and goes with the Fremen into the deep desert. A Fremen rides a worm in
   the distance.

## What the show keeps, and what it cuts

The show keeps the film's order and the beats the brief names. Its cast is locked: Paul, Jessica, Leto, the Reverend
Mother, the Baron, Chani, Stilgar, Jamis, the Fremen, Harkonnen soldiers, and the harvester's crew. The cut:

- the herald's ceremony, Gurney, Thufir, the training with the shield;
- the hunter-seeker;
- Giedi Prime;
- Yueh as a person (his betrayal is the shield going out);
- Kynes and Duncan (the storm needs only the escape);
- the visions of the holy war: Paul's visions in the show are the show's own later frames, so what he sees is what
  will happen in it.

## The film's look

- **Format.** Shot for IMAX on large-format digital, transferred to 35 mm film and scanned back for a filmic grain.
  Some scenes open to 1.90:1 on every IMAX screen and 1.43:1 on dual-laser IMAX. The show keeps Caladan in the old scope
  picture and opens the frame as the frigate comes down over Arrakis.
- **Caladan** was designed after autumn in Canada, with Norwegian pines, and shot on the Stadlandet coast in Norway.
  Its interiors draw on medieval Japanese building. So the show's Caladan is grey-green sea, black rock and dark stone
  outside, with timber, paper screens and lamps within.
- **Arrakis** was shot in Wadi Rum in Jordan, Liwa Oasis in the UAE, and the Rub' al Khali. Sandscreens stood in for
  green screens, so the light is the desert's own. Arrakeen is a rock bowl with angled buildings and thick walls.
- **Costume** was grounded in history. The Fremen were tied to the French Resistance, the Sardaukar to the Nazis and
  the Atreides to the Romanovs. The Harkonnen armour was made to look like a bug's shell.
- **The worm** was designed as a prehistoric creature, after whales.
