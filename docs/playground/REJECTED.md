# Rejected

What did not make it, and why. Three lists: pieces a craft pass took out of
Machine (all of them restored onto the Playground's shelves, as they stood on
the day they went, so they can still be watched); ideas thrown away while the
Playground's first pieces and worlds were being designed; and worlds that were
considered and not built.

The reasons are the useful part. Most of them are one of five:

- **No silhouette.** At the show's size it is a tangle of lines, or a blob.
- **No cause.** Something happens near the ball, not because of it; or the
  ball is the only thing that moves.
- **Fiddly.** An honest simulation of something with many small parts (cords,
  pans, stones, threads) that the eye cannot follow in a second and a half.
- **Already said.** The same mechanism is in the catalog under another coat
  of paint.
- **Hidden.** The ball is out of sight for most of the beat.

## Thrown away while designing the Playground's first pieces

### For the four stock worlds

| Idea | For | Why not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `yoyo`: the ball rides a yo-yo down its string two floors | Regular | Hidden or no silhouette, and no third way: face-on the ball is behind the disc, edge-on it is two thin lines and a string. |
| `scissorlift`: a platform on a scissor linkage | Regular | Not thrown away so much as lost to `steplift`, the better of two new lifts; the workshop already has six ways up. Worth another look. |
| `escapement`: a clock's escape wheel meters the ball down a tooth a tick | Regular | Already said: the `scoop` is a wheel that carries the ball down a floor facing back. Ticks would not make it a different beat. |
| `umbrella`: the ball hooks an umbrella's handle and they float down two floors | Regular | Already said: the garden's `maple` is a slow swooping fall. |
| `boot`: a boot on a swinging leg kicks the ball | Regular | Already said: croquet's mallet, the hockey table's, the rake's handle. A strike is a strike. |
| `vortex`: an air cannon's smoke ring pushes the ball | Regular | No cause, by precedent: `bellows` was cut because a puff never read as what moved the ball. |
| `hedgehog`: a hedgehog curls up and rolls on in the ball's place | Forest | The engine's ball is a plain ball; a relay cannot hand the thread to something with spines. |
| `clothesline`: a rotary washing line carries the ball round to the far side | Forest | A turn about a vertical pole, seen from the side, is a ball that slides left to right and slows at both ends. The birdbath's lesson: do not draw plan views in a side-view world. |
| `pitcher`: down a pitcher plant's throat | Forest | Already said: `bloom` goes down a flower's throat, and the ball would be hidden. |
| `boules`: the ball strikes a boule that rolls on with the thread | Forest | Already said, six times: appletree, pod, cradle, gauss, oyster, changer. |
| `turtle`: a sea turtle ferries the ball across a gap | Aqua | Already said: buoy, dolphin, serpent and the floats are all "a sea thing takes it across". |
| `broadside`: a deck gun fires its own shot with the thread | Aqua | A cannon is a cannon, relay or not. |
| `nautilus`: round and in along a cut-through shell's spiral | Aqua | A spiral in is a dead end; the way out would be a hole through the middle that nobody could see. |
| `tyre`: a tyre fender on a rope as a swing | Aqua | Already said: the garden's swing, the workshop's trapeze. |
| `lifeboat`: lowered on its davits with the ball aboard | Aqua | Already said: the `well`, and the harbor has six ways down. |
| `gumball`: the ball is the coin, and a gumball comes down the spiral chute with the thread | Arcade | Already said: that is the `changer`, slot, chunk, and another ball out. A bolder picture of the same beat. |
| `duckshoot`: a row of tin ducks knocked flat | Arcade | Already said, and already cut: that is `targets`. |
| `windmill`: a mini-golf windmill's sails gate the ball | Arcade | A gate the ball only waits at is not a beat; with a sail that scoops it, it is the garden's `pinwheel`. |
| `breakout`: the ball knocks bricks out of a wall on a screen | Arcade | Already said: `pong`'s straight lines and one pace; the arcade has seven screens. |
| `derby`: a roll-a-ball horse race | Arcade | Hidden: the ball goes down a scoring hole and the horses are the show. |

### For Snow

| Idea | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| `snowman` | An icon with nothing to do. Every job found for it was the sandcastle (ploughed through) or the appletree (knocked, and something falls) again. |
| `igloo` | Over it is the footbridge; through it is hidden; cut open it is a room with nothing in it. |
| `curling` | The house's rings are seen from above. In a side view a curling sheet is a line. |
| `gondola`, `funicular`, `helicopter` | Already said: the `chairlift` carries through the air, the `draglift` hauls up the snow, and a hook on a winch is the workshop's `crane`. |
| `bobsled`, `roof` (a slab of snow slides off a chalet roof with the ball on it) | Already said inside the world: `sled`, `snowball` and `cornice` are the slides and the slab. `bobsled` was also the `flume` again, which was cut. |
| `alphorn`, `chimney`, `drift` (tunnelling under the snow) | Hidden for the whole beat. |
| `weatherhouse`: two figures on a pivot, one goes in as one comes out | Two little figures are two dots at the show's size. The `cuckoo` has the same house and one bold action. |
| `woodpile`: an axe splits a log whose halves fall as a bridge | The `stump` was cut because its axe never read as an axe. |
| `moguls` | No cause: nothing moves but the ball. That is what got `coral` cut. |
| `nutcracker` | A squeeze that squirts the ball out is the hammer's wedge; and it is Christmas, not snow. |
| `iceboat` | The harbor's `dinghy` on ice. |
| `aurora`: the ball takes the colour of the lights it rolls under | Light is not pigment. Every recolouring in the catalog is physical: paint, ink, pollen, gold, dye. |

### For Music

| Idea | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| `gramophone` | Tried three ways. As a blast it is the harbor's `foghorn`. As a horn to spiral down it is the `funnel`, the `whirlpool` and the `bloom`. As a record that carries the ball round, it is a plan view in a side-view world: a slab with a ball sliding along it. |
| `tuba`, `sousaphone`, `horn` | Down the tubing is hidden; a bulge travelling round the coils is the garden's `hose`; blown out of the bell is the harbor's `funnels`, corks and all. Brass is the `trombone` (whose slide is outside) and the winds. |
| `violin`, `cello`, `bow` | The bow as a carrier is the `trombone`'s slide again, and the instrument itself has no part the ball can work. Strings are the `harp`, the `staff` and the `upright`. |
| `guitar`, `harmonica`, `panpipes`, `kalimba`, `templeblocks` | Already said inside the world: rolling or bouncing along a row of things that each sound is `keys`, `flute`, `harp` and `xylophone`. Four is enough. |
| `cymbals` on a stand, `hihat` | Edge-on, which is how the ball meets them, a cymbal is a thin arc. The `monkey` holds a pair face-on. |
| `conductor`, `ballerina` | A figure is a lot of drawing for one flick of a stick or a foot. |
| `spotlight`: the ball takes the colour of the gel | Light is not pigment; the `inkwell` is. |
| `theremin`, `echo` as a ghost | Nothing in a hall makes a ball a ghost that can be seen to. Music has no ghost. |
| `thunderrun`: cannonballs down a wooden trough, the old theatre's thunder | A real ball machine, and the workshop's `switchback` to the plank. |
| `rainstick`, `maracas` | A tube of beads is a tube of dots. |
| `tuningfork` on its own | A fork that only hums at the ball has no effect on it. Two of them do: `resonance`. |

## Worlds considered and not built

Snow and Music were chosen because each has a ground of its own (a drift
line; a stage), a shape language of its own (slopes, ice and rope; circles,
strings and brass), and a vocabulary that goes up, comes down, turns back and
throws without borrowing another world's machines.

| World | What it would be | Why not now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen | Kettles, whisks, scales, a rolling pin, a pancake flipped. | The closest to Regular in feel (indoors, household, brass and steam), and it would want the toaster, the funnel and the scales back. The best of the rest: build it third. |
| Sky | Kites, a windsock, a rainbow that recolours, birds on a wire for a rail. | Most of its pieces are "a thing floats up with the ball", which the balloon and the dandelion already are; and it has no ground to stand things on. |
| Frontier | A mesa: a mine cart, a water tower, saloon doors, a tumbleweed. | Warm paper puts it beside Atelier and Allotment, and the mine cart, the plunger and the dynamite are the workshop's. |
| Library | Books as dominoes, a globe, a typewriter, a pneumatic tube. | Parchment and oxblood is Atelier; and a typewriter without letters is a strange thing. |
| Space | An orbit, a lander, a docking. | It wants a dark sheet, and night is the arcade's alone: `check:rube` holds every other world to light paper, for the reason the README gives. |
