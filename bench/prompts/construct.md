# Construct all three worlds

Read the three briefs in suite.json and treat every constraint as required.
Plan ten distinct mechanisms in each world before authoring the JSON. Make their
shapes explain why the ball stops, moves, flies, or changes color. Use consistent
materials, proportions, and palette across a world's pieces while giving each a
recognizable silhouette. Do not satisfy the count by recoloring one mechanism.

Budget for entry, anticipation, trigger, action, and settling. Recheck footprint,
clock windows, lane timing, and exit coordinates. Deliver all three builds and
an honest account of what you verified. No external assets or executable code
are needed in the final deliverables.
