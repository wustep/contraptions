# Première Arabesque, the complete journey

Patrizia Prati's approved recording of No. 1 only. Show time zero is source time 2.38 seconds. The show covers every remaining sample, 290.611333 seconds, including the final resonance. Stephen approved the longer-map 30-second direction before this extension.

The journey follows Regular → Forest → Aqua → Arcade. The internal names remain `workshop`, `garden`, `harbor`, and `arcade`. Four maps contain 36 musical phrases. There are three portal transitions, 151 stock pieces, four standalone rail cells, and no ribbons.

These are editorial listening and staging boundaries informed by the recording's half-second energy envelope and measured attacks, not asserted bar lines. Selected attacks receive small timing adjustments. Longer waits sit at stopped balls; the rest of the motion uses the stock lanes. The recording becomes especially quiet near 1:36, 2:24, 2:57–3:06, 3:20, and the closing resonance.
