On the Nature of Daylight — Max Richter
From The Blue Notebooks (130701 / FatCat Records, 2004); the recording Arrival opens and closes on.
Composed by Max Richter; the album's own recording (strings), as the label released it.
Film: Arrival, directed by Denis Villeneuve, Paramount Pictures, 2016; screenplay by Eric Heisserer, from Ted Chiang's
"Story of Your Life" (1998). The film's soundtrack album is by Jóhann Jóhannsson (Deutsche Grammophon, 2016); this cue
is Richter's own recording, used in the film's opening and closing scenes.

The recording is the album's own, as uploaded to YouTube by the label (FatCat Records' channel, "Max Richter - On the
Nature of Daylight", "Taken from 'Max Richter: The Blue Notebooks'"), video rVN1B-tUpgs:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rVN1B-tUpgs
