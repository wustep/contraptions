# Sleeper

`/shows/pauls-dream/opus55/` · **Sleeper**, one take, **Opus 5.5**, on the Movies shelf.

**Sleeper · after Dune.** Hans Zimmer's *Paul's Dream* is from *Dune (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)* (WaterTower
Music, 2021), the film's main theme and its first single. Here it is played whole by YouTube from WaterTower Music's
own upload (BdtiYwSP9ko), from its first sample, and then the end credits run on into the quiet after it: 440 s in all.
No recording is shipped (`ATTRIBUTION.txt`). Why this cue, and its measured structure, are in
`docs/shows/sleeper/cue.md`; the research behind the beats is in `docs/shows/sleeper/research.md`.

*The sleeper must awaken* is the novel's line, and the first trailer's.

## The idea

Paul spends Part One between sleep and waking. On Caladan he dreams of a desert and a girl in it; in the spice he sees
what is coming; in the desert he meets her. So the show's visions are its own future: when Paul dreams, a soft opening
grows from him onto a later frame of the show itself, playing. Asleep in Castle Caladan he sees Chani on a crest at
dawn, the shot the show will reach four minutes later; in the spice by the harvester he sees it again; in the stilltent
he sees the duel he will fight and the worm rider he will watch. When the show gets there, the dream comes true in the
same frame.

The colour arc is the dream's: Caladan is grey-green, sea and rain and dark stone, its only warm things the castle's
lamps, and the ochre of Arrakis comes into it first through his dreams. Caladan is in the old scope picture between
black bars; as the frigate comes down over Arrakis the frame opens, as Villeneuve opens his picture to the IMAX screen's
full height in the desert, and stays open.

**The cast is locked.** Everyone who is a person is a ball:
- Paul is sea green, the green of Caladan's water; he carries it into the desert, the one thing of the sea left there.
- Jessica is plum, the Bene Gesserit's dark.
- Leto, the Duke, is Atreides slate, bigger than his son.
- The Reverend Mother is black, bigger again, and only at the box.
- The Baron is pale and swollen, the biggest, and he floats.
- Chani is henna. Stilgar is dark umber, Jamis a darker umber, and the Fremen the umber of their cloaks.
- The Harkonnen soldiers are gunmetal; the harvester's crew dust grey.

Everything else is drawn: the castle, the box, the bull's head, the frigate and the heighliner, the ornithopters, the
harvester, the worms, the palace, the armada, the stilltent, the storm.

**One job per prop:**
- The sea is the world he leaves.
- The box is the test: his hand stays in it while its seam burns.
- The gom jabbar is death if he takes it out.
- The bull's head is the father's line: it looks down on Leto from Caladan's hall to his death in Arrakeen's.
- The frigate and the heighliner carry the house; the fold hands the frigate from one sky to the other.
- The ornithopter is flight; its wings beat on the click.
- The harvester is the spice, and the carryall that never comes is its absence.
- The worm is the desert's power.
- The shield is the house's safety, and it goes out.
- The tooth is Leto's last blow.
- The stilltent keeps them alive a night, and the spice in its air opens the visions.
- The storm is the only way out.
- The worm rider is what Paul is going toward.

## How it is made

`pauls-dream/sleeper/`:
- `show.ts` is one world on one clock (`SleeperShow`): Caladan and Arrakis side by side in one plane, the balls of the
  cast each on a run of its own (`path.ts`: rests, rolls, true-gravity hops, rides). The stage draws what the show
  lists (`ShowPoint.balls`); a mark turns only as its ball rolls, or turns to look (`looks`).
- `scene.ts` and `plan.ts` lay the film out as scenes, each owning a window of show time, and say what the scenes
  promise each other at their seams (the fold, the roof pad, the dream shot, the duel's fall, the rider). `score.ts`
  lays them end to end.
- `camera.ts` is an authored camera: holds and tracks on monotone cubics, cuts only on the music, dissolves at the
  changes of place.
- The visions are `Performance.blend` pointed forward: a later moment of the show, playing, laid over the frame
  through a soft opening that grows from Paul (`Dream` in `scene.ts`).
- `film.ts` is the frame's bars (`band`: a phone held upright keeps its whole 16:9 frame between them).
- `world/desert.ts` is Arrakis's air and sand, shared by every desert scene; `craft/` the ornithopter, the frigate and
  the bull's head, shared by the scenes that meet them.
- `scenes/` holds the places: `caladan/`, `arrakeen/` (the arrival and the night), `erg/`, `storm/`, `fremen/`.

The look is flat fills and few lines. Lines never fall under 1.25 CSS pixels. Depth comes from layers paling toward the
air (`layer()`: a dolly camera, a far layer `h / (h + d)` of its size). Light only from where it comes from, as flat
shapes. There is no glow, bloom or sparkle, and nothing is written on the frame. The end credits are the page's
(`credits.ts`), painted into the frame only in a saved video; on a phone they keep the house floor (`least` 4.5) and
rise into the sky over the frame (`lift` 0.6).

## The clock

`scripts/shows/pauls-dream-onsets.py` measures the cue once into `scripts/shows/plans/pauls-dream-onsets.json`: its
stretches and landmarks, every onset with its strength and low share, the low swells and booms, the click where it is
held, loudness and the high voices. `apps/rube/checks/sleeper.ts` holds:
- every strike the story turns on to that file (each scene's `strikes`), and every bare camera cut;
- Caladan to the scope picture and the frame opening on the drop;
- Chani in the dream shot through the whole of it;
- every ball to never jumping, coming or going in shot, but at a cut or under a blend;
- the credits and the soundtrack to what they owe.

## How it was made

The kit (the clock, paths, the camera, the plan, the desert, the ornithopter, the frigate, the bull's head, the bars)
and Caladan were the director's; Arrakeen, the erg, the storm and the Fremen were built in parallel by four builders in
their own worktrees to a contract (each scene's window, its seams, the cast it owns, the look's rules), and integrated
by copying their folders.

- **First build.** Every scene end to end on the music.
