## What this breaks, on purpose

Clair Take B is a catalog tour: four worlds, almost no repeated machines, rails as breath, twelve named cues. This demo is a pulse piece, about two minutes, so it breaks those rules:

- Two worlds, not four. Forest is the field. The Arcade is the chase. One portal, on the drop at the downbeat, not mid-phrase.
- Repeats are the ostinato. Bumpers, pins and the other short Arcade gestures answer the beat instead of each appearing once.
- The field is sparse (about every five beats) so the long machines can be seen before they fire. The chase is a strike on almost every beat of the 95.7 bpm grid.
- The camera opens up while the piano is alone and tightens when the organ is running. Overview is for looking at a whole map; the recording is Zoom.
- The Arcade score pops are on. The ticket pays what this chase earned.
