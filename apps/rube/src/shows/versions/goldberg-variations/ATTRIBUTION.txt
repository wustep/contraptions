Composition: Johann Sebastian Bach, Aria mit verschiedenen Veränderungen (Goldberg Variations), BWV 988 (published 1741).
Public domain.

embedded player to play the album's own uploads (the "Víkingur Ólafsson – Topic" channel's one video to a track, 32 of
them), and the sound comes from YouTube, not from this site. The show links the album:
