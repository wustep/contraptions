Raindrop (versions/raindrop/opus55.show.ts)

Music
  Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849), Prelude in D-flat major, Op. 28 No. 15 ("Raindrop"), from the 24 Preludes, Op. 28,
  composed 1838–39 (finished at the Charterhouse of Valldemossa, Mallorca) and first published in 1839.
  Composition: public domain.

Recording
  "Chopin: Prelude no. 15 in D flat major", played by Ivan Ilić, recorded in Paris, October 2005, and released by him
  on the International Music Score Library Project (IMSLP).
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivan_Ili%C4%87-Chopin_-_Prelude_no._15_in_D_flat_major.ogg
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes: the Commons file (Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, 263.58 s) is re-encoded whole to chopin-raindrop-ilic.mp3 (MP3,
  44.1 kHz, 160 kbit/s, constant bit rate, so that a seek lands where the show's clock says) by
  scripts/shows/raindrop-cue.sh. Nothing is cut, faded or levelled.

The score, for where its notes fall
  The Mutopia Project's engraving of the prelude (Chop-28-15), read as MIDI by scripts/shows/raindrop-onsets.py to
  lay the score on the recording. Nothing of it ships; scripts/shows/plans/raindrop-onsets.json holds times only.

Contraptions audiovisual adaptation: a timed machine drawn over the whole recording. The credit is given beside the
player and in the show's end credits and documentation, never on the frame. The recording ships with the show under
its licence; the adaptation is shared under CC BY 4.0.
