Soft Lamp — the music

Lofi Girl, "Best of lofi hip hop 2021 ✨ [beats to relax/study to]", the label's own upload to YouTube:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n61ULEU7CO0
The show plays its first twelve tracks, from the video's zero to 30:12.4, from that upload only.

  1  morning moon — Kanisan, Wishes and Dreams
  2  Lavender — Kupla
  3  Destination Unknown — amies
  4  Overgrown — Tenno
  5  Magical Connection — Peak Twilight, Prithvi
  6  Blooming Dales — Krynoze, Diiolme
  7  Exhale — No Spirit
  8  Stargazing — kyu
  9  Breathtaking — Purrple Cat
  10 takeoff — stream_error
  11 Daydream — kyu
  12 Passing By — Casiio, Sleepermane

Released by Lofi Girl on the compilation "Best of lofi hip hop 2021" (2021). The tracklist and artists are the album's
own. Copyrighted; no rights are claimed. The compilation's artwork and animation (Margaux Peltat, Andreas Provoost)
are not used: every picture in the show is drawn for it.

Playback: YouTube only. No recording of the mix is shipped, and none is committed.

Files:
- scripts/shows/soft-lamp-onsets.py — measures the mix once from a local analysis copy of the video's first forty
  minutes (fetched with yt-dlp into the git-ignored out/soft-lamp/, never committed): each track's edges, its grid,
  its downbeats, its drum bars, its kicks and cracks, and the loudness and held sound.
- scripts/shows/plans/soft-lamp-onsets.json — what it measured: the show is timed to it and check:shows holds the show
  to it. It holds times and numbers only, no audio.
