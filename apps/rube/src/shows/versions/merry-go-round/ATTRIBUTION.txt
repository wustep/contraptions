Merry-Go-Round of Life (Jinsei no Merry-Go-Round) — Joe Hisaishi
From Howl's Moving Castle (Hauru no Ugoku Shiro), 2004. Music composed by Joe Hisaishi.
Film: Howl's Moving Castle, written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki, Studio Ghibli, 2004; from the novel by
Diana Wynne Jones (1986).

build, the climax a key higher, the last tutti), as uploaded to the artist's official, verified channel "Joe
Hisaishi Official" as "Joe Hisaishi - Merry-Go-Round of Life (from Howl's Moving Castle)", linked to the album
Dream Songs: The Essential Joe Hisaishi (Decca Gold / Universal Music, 2020):
