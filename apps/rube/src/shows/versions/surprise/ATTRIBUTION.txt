Music
  Joseph Haydn (1732–1809), Symphony No. 94 in G major, Hob. I:94, "Surprise" ("mit dem Paukenschlag"), second
  movement, Andante, composed 1791 for London. Composition: public domain.

Recording
  "Haydn: Symphony No. 94 in G Major, Hob. I:94 "Surprise": II. Andante", Berliner Philharmoniker, Herbert von
  Karajan, from "Haydn: Symphonies Nos. 94 "Surprise" & 101 "The Clock"", ℗ 1982 Deutsche Grammophon GmbH, Berlin.
  As uploaded to YouTube by the label's distributor ("Provided to YouTube by Universal Music Group"), on the
  Berliner Philharmoniker's channel, video 14G_PDZWXVE:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=14G_PDZWXVE
  Duration 5:40. Copyrighted; no rights are claimed.

Playback: YouTube only. No recording of the movement is shipped, and none is committed, in this branch or its history.

Files:
- scripts/shows/surprise-onsets.py — measures the movement once from a local analysis copy of the video's audio
  (fetched with yt-dlp into the git-ignored out/surprise/, never committed): every strain's own comb, every beat on its
  own attack, the onsets, the surprise, the last chord, the loudness.
- scripts/shows/plans/surprise-onsets.json — what it measured: the show is timed to it and check:shows holds the show
  to it. It holds times and numbers only, no audio.

Every picture in the show is drawn for it. Nothing from the album's sleeve, the label's artwork or any score's
engraving is used.
