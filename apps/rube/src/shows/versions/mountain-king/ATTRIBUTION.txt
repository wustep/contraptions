Mountain King (versions/mountain-king/opus55.show.ts)

Music
  Edvard Grieg (1843–1907), "In the Hall of the Mountain King"
  Peer Gynt Suite No. 1, Op. 46, IV (from the incidental music to Ibsen's Peer Gynt, Op. 23, 1875)
  Composition: public domain.

Recording
  Czech National Symphony Orchestra, recorded for Musopen (the Musopen Kickstarter project, 2012).
  Released by Musopen into the public domain: "This work has been released into the public domain by its author,
  Musopen. This applies worldwide." Musopen grants anyone the right to use this work for any purpose, without any
  conditions.
  Source file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grieg_-_Peer_Gynt_Suite_No._1,_Op._46_-_IV._In_the_Hall_of_the_Mountain_King_(Musopen_Symphony).flac
  (FLAC, 48 kHz, 154.091 s), re-encoded whole and untouched to grieg-mountain-king-musopen.mp3
  (160 kbit/s) by scripts/shows/mountain-king-cue.sh.

  The show can also play the same recording from YouTube: the orchestra's label upload,
  "Czech National Symphony Orchestra, Prague - Topic" (provided to YouTube by CDBaby, from the compilation
  "50 Masterpieces of Classical Music", which credits Libor Pešek as conductor),
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k8HCJS4FflY. Measured against the Musopen file it is the same recording,
  sample for sample (lag 0, the same 154.091 s).

Story
  After Henrik Ibsen, Peer Gynt (1867), Act Two: the hall of the Dovre King (public domain).

The file is shipped with the show: it is public domain, not a demo-only recording.
