Near Light — Ólafur Arnalds
From Living Room Songs (2011), Erased Tapes Records (ERATP037). Composed by Ólafur Arnalds.
Performed by Ólafur Arnalds (piano, electronics programming, recording, mixing and mastering), Roland Hartwell and
Margrét Soffía Einarsdóttir (violins), Arndís Hulda Auðunsdóttir (viola), Karl Jóhann Bjarnason (cello), Fríða Ósk
Arnalds (Juno 106 and drum loop) and Guðrún Pálmadóttir (Korg MS-10), as the Living Room Songs film credits them.

Living Room Songs by Ólafur Arnalds"), video ejaaxLeUQd4:
